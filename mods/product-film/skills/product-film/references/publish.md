# Review files in FactoryOS

The upstream skill published review pages on display.dev. FactoryOS removed that path (`scripts/publish.sh`,
`scripts/_common.sh`, the bundled jq binaries and the `dsp` tools), because nothing may leave FactoryOS without the
owner's approval.

Instead:

1. Keep `review/review-vN.html`, the 720p MP4 and the poster in the film folder.
2. Report their paths and a four-frame contact sheet per changed beat in your final answer.
3. The owner scores the cut in FactoryOS (Mods & skills → video duel). Publication, if any, goes through the
   FactoryOS approval gate, never from this skill.

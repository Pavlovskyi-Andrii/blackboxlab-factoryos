# Verification C: AI vendor terms and prices (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) for running agents on subscriptions

Checked 2026-11-18 by an adversarial fact-checker. Method: WebSearch restricted to official domains, WebFetch and `curl` of github.com and raw.githubusercontent.com, and the bundled `claude-api` skill (model table cached 2026-11-03). The shared web-search budget ran out after 4 searches, so anthropic.com news posts, ai.google.dev, help.openai.com and developers.openai.com could not be checked. Several claims combine a checked part with an unchecked part, so they are split into two rows (for example C1 and C1-b). Researcher notes in this folder were used only to find the URLs the researchers cited, never as evidence.

**Overall reliability:** The core claims hold up well against primary sources (C1, C2, C3, C5, C7 and C8 confirmed; C4 needs the Haiku 5.5 long-context price tier). Several precise dates could not be checked against a primary source: 4 April 2026, 6 May 2026, 7 October 2026 and 26 April 2026. The Google price-change figures in C6 also remain unverifiable. C1-b may describe a superseded state, so the report should not present the April 2026 cut-off as current policy.

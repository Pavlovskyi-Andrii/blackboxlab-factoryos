Checked 2026-11-18 by an adversarial fact-checker. Research only: nothing in the product was changed.

**Method limits.** Only 5 web searches ran before the workflow's shared search budget (200 calls per turn) ran out. Those 5 covered B1-B3. After that, primary sources could only be reached on GitHub and PyPI: Google's own YouTube API discovery document, X's own developer-docs repository and Metricool's own PyPI package. B5, B6 and B8 could not be checked against the platforms' own pages. "Search extract" means the WebSearch result was restricted to the official domain and the full page was not opened.

**Overall reliability:** B1-B3 hold up against the official domains. B7 is right in substance but the price date is 20 Apr 2026, not "early 2026", and free credits exist. B4 is half confirmed from Google's own API description; the half that matters here (no forced-private lock) also shows that the repo's `DECISIONS.md` is stale. B5, B6 and B8 rest on secondary repo notes and need a re-check on the platforms' own pages before anyone acts on them. B8's "MCP on Free" conflicts with Metricool's own package README.
